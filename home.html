How it works

From sign-up to a running project in three steps. Only the Client creates the project account.

Client creates the project

Register your company, enter the project, contractor and consultant details, and confirm with an email verification code.

Each party joins

The contractor and consultant receive a secure invitation and set their own password on first login.

Every side builds its team

Each party assigns its Project Manager and team members, who work from role-based dashboards.

Everything a project needs, on record

Built around how construction projects are actually run and who has to answer for what.

Document approval & stamping

Submit, review, resubmit and approve with full version history. Approved documents carry digital stamps anyone can verify.

Progress against plan

Milestone schedules, actual progress with site photos, extension requests with reasons, and live variance and remaining days.

Site verification & rework

Inspections, accept-or-reject decisions and rework tracking until closed out, so quality issues never get lost.

Stop-work (ARRET) notices

Raise formal stop-work notices with evidence attached. Every party is alerted and sees who has viewed them.

Work Center & tasks

Project Managers assign tasks, members submit progress, and queries are raised, answered and resolved in one thread.

Meetings, records & chat

Schedule meetings with RSVP and calendar export, produce PDF minutes, keep categorised project records and chat in real time.

One platform, three parties

Each party has its own Project Manager, team and permissions, while sharing one project record.

Clients

Own the project, set the schedule, review progress, approve extensions and see everything in one dashboard.

Consultants

Review submissions, issue instructions, inspect work and keep an auditable record of advice and decisions.

Contractors

Report progress with photos, submit documents for approval, request extensions and coordinate site teams.
