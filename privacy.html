1. Who we are

OneProjectApp is a construction project collaboration platform operated by [legal name of operator, registered address, country]. This policy explains what personal information we handle when you visit the site or use the platform.

2. What we collect

  • Account details – name, company or individual name, email, telephone, job title or position, role on the project, and an optional profile picture.
  • Project information – project name, location, contract reference, and details of the contractor, consultant and team members entered by users.
  • Content you add – documents, drawings, reports, site photos, tasks, progress updates, meeting details and minutes, stop-work (ARRET) notices, comments and chat messages, together with approval, review, signing and stamping history.
  • Sign-in and security data – your password (stored in hashed form), verification and reset codes, and sign-in tokens.
  • Service data – language preference, notifications, and email delivery status for messages we send you.

3. How we use it

  • To create accounts, verify email addresses and sign you in.
  • To run the project workspace: documents, approvals, progress tracking, meetings, tasks and chat.
  • To send invitations, verification codes, reset codes and project notifications by email and in the app.
  • To keep the platform secure, prevent misuse and keep an auditable record of project decisions.
  • To show project weather for the project location.

We do not sell personal information.

4. Who can see your information

Projects are shared by design. Other people on the same project – the Client, Consultant and Contractor and their project managers and team members – can see project records and the names, emails and roles of project participants, according to their role permissions. Some work, such as a team's own workspace and meetings, is visible only to that side. Your profile picture is visible to everyone on the same project. People outside the project cannot access it. A stamped document can be verified by anyone holding its verification link, and the verification page confirms the stamp status.

6. Retention and deletion

  • When a Client schedules deletion of a project, it becomes read-only for a 60-day recovery period and can be restored during that time. After that, the project and its stored files are permanently deleted. This does not delete the Client account or other projects.
  • A member who leaves a project has a 15-day exit period; their earlier contributions remain in the project record.

7. Security

Access is controlled by role. Passwords are hashed, email verification is required for key steps, and connections use HTTPS. No online service is completely secure, so please use a strong, unique password and sign out on shared devices.

8. Your choices

  • Update your profile details and picture, or delete your picture, from your profile panel.
  • Reset your password at any time from the login window or Settings.
  • Ask us to access, correct or delete your personal information, or object to certain uses, by contacting us below. Project records are shared with other participants, so some information may need to stay where the project requires it.

9. Browser storage

We use your browser's local storage to keep you signed in, remember the active project and your language choice, and carry information between the steps of account creation. We do not use it for advertising. You can clear it in your browser settings, which will sign you out.
